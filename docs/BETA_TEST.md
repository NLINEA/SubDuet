# SubDuet 10-minute beta mission

The goal is simple: see one SubDuet result, then try one result with your own files. No analytics
or SubDuet account is involved.

## 1. Check the app safely

1. Download the archive for your computer from the
   [latest beta release](https://github.com/NLINEA/SubDuet/releases).
2. Unzip it and open **SubDuet**.
3. Choose the player you use.
4. Choose **Try safe demo**.

Success means a tiny English-Spanish `.mul.srt` appears in your Downloads folder. Its dialogue was
written for this project. SubDuet does not use a server, key, network request, or your media for
this step.

## 2. Try your own result

The shortest route is **Choose two SRTs**. Select the subtitle spoken in the video first and the
language you want to read or learn second. SubDuet keeps both files on your device, combines them,
and highlights the new `.mul.srt`. Both original files remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

If you only have a video, choose **Try one video** instead. SubDuet will explain any optional search,
translation, transcription, or FFmpeg requirement before asking for it.

Success means your player can select the bilingual `.mul.srt` beside the video. If it does not
appear, make its base filename match the video while keeping the `.mul.srt` ending.

## 3. Report only the outcome

Use the short [first-result form](https://github.com/NLINEA/SubDuet/issues/new?template=beta_report.yml).
Tell us the platform, the two language names, whether you succeeded without help, and the most
confusing step.

Do not include credentials, private paths, media titles, logs with personal data, screenshots of
your library, or subtitle text. SubDuet sends nothing automatically; opening and submitting the
GitHub form is your choice.
